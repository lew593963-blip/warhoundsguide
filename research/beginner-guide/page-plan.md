# Page Plan — Beginner Guide

- Page ID: `beginner-guide`
- Priority: P0
- Pilot: yes
- Search intent: give a new player one evidence-safe mental model for the tactical and strategic layers.
- Primary answer: protect role coverage, solve the objective before chasing eliminations, treat information and positioning as resources, and make base spending answer the next mission risk.
- Required sections: quick start, campaign loop, first squad, tactical turn, base priorities, choices, common mistakes, FAQ.
- Evidence boundary: official Steam listing and official site only; all recommendations must be labeled as practical inference.
- Internal links: squad guide, base upgrades, weapons and combat guide.
- Exclusions: invented tutorial steps, exact hidden formulas, unsupported meta tier lists, mission-specific walkthroughs.
- Success condition: a reader can make coherent early decisions without the page pretending to know unpublished rules.
