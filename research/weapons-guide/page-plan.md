# Page Plan — Weapons & Combat

- Page ID: `weapons-guide`
- Priority: P0
- Merged cluster: `warhounds combat guide` (P1)
- Search intent: help players choose weapons and tools by tactical job, then use terrain, sight lines, ballistics, and overwatch coherently.
- Required sections: evidence boundary, weapon-job matrix, named tools, positioning loop, destructible cover, high ground, overwatch, loadout checklist, FAQ.
- Evidence boundary: official sources name systems and tools but do not publish full tables or a safe universal tier list.
- Internal links: beginner, squad, base.
- Exclusions: invented damage values, exact hit formulas, complete gun list, unsupported best-weapon rankings.
- Success condition: the reader can choose a loadout by objective and understand how equipment interacts with positioning.
